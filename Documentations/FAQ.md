# FAQ

**Question:**\n How can I track the payment status of all students?\n **Answer:**\n You can track the payment status only if the **Dues Management** payment mode is selected. The platform provides full visibility of payment records.


**Question:**\n How do I allocate rooms if a student selects Offline Payment?\n **Answer:**\n **Offline Payment** is manually tracked. You must confirm the payment externally and allocate rooms manually. **Auto Allotment** is not available with **Offline Payment**.


**Question:**\n Can I select multiple departments for the hostel policy option?\n **Answer:**\n Yes, the **Hostel Administrator** can select multiple departments using checkboxes under **Hostel Policy Options**.


**Question:**\n How can I ensure that room allocation follows institutional policies?\n **Answer:**\n Use **Hostel Policy Options** with predefined criteria like gender, batch year, and department. This ensures room allocation aligns with institutional policies.


**Question:**\n How can I manage Auto Allotment if Offline Payment is selected?\n **Answer:**\n **Auto Allotment** does not apply for **Offline Payment**. You must manually allocate rooms once the payment is confirmed externally.


**Question:**\n How can I check if my hostel fee payment has been processed?\n **Answer:**\n You can check your payment status in the **Payments** section on the platform under **Dues Management**.


**Question:**\n What should I do if I need to change my room after it's been allocated?\n **Answer:**\n Contact the **Hostel Administrator** to check room availability and request a change if needed, subject to availability and institutional policies.


**Question:**\n Can I select my preferred room?\n **Answer:**\n Yes, if **Dues Management** is selected, you can select your preferred room and make payment.


**Question:**\n How do I make a payment for my hostel fees?\n **Answer:**\n You can make payments through **Dues Management** on the platform or through **Offline Payment** enabled by the institution.



---

**Question:**\n Will my room be automatically allocated once I make my payment?\n **Answer:**\n Yes, if **Dues Management** is selected, your room will be allocated automatically once your payment is cleared through the platform.


**Question:**\n What happens if I don't pay my fees through **Dues Management**?\n **Answer:**\n If you don't pay through **Dues Management**, your room allocation will be delayed until your payment is processed and confirmed.
