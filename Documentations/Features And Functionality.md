# Features And Functionality

## __Prerequisites__ 


The following admin privileges have to be provided to admins or staffs to access the Hostel Management Configuration module :-



1. **Hostel Management Settings Super Admin Write** : ""Hostel Management Settings Super Admin Write" admin privilege allows wardens to access and modify the hostel management configuration settings which includes payment and allotment type.

   \
2. **Hostel Management Settings Super Admin Read** : "Hostel Management Settings Super Admin Read" admin privilege allows wardens to only view and access the hostel management configuration settings which mainly includes payment and allotment type.

| Hostel Management Configuration | Hostel Management Configuration | This feature allows institutions to configure how hostel rooms are assigned to students and how fee payments are processed. It includes the following configurations:<br>Hostel Payment Mode: This setting allows institutions to define how hostel fee payments are processed. There are two options:<br>Dues Management: All payment details, including fee amount, due date, and payment status, are tracked within the platform, offering complete visibility to both students and Hostel Administrators.<br>Offline Payment: Payments are made through traditional methods such as cash or cheque, and these are managed externally by the institution. The platform does not track these payments.<br>Hostel Allotment Type: This configuration determines how hostel rooms are assigned to students. Based on the chosen Hostel Payment Mode, institutions can select one of the following allotment types:<br>Manual Allotment: Under this allotment type, Hostel Administrators personally assign rooms to students. When Dues Management is selected, room allocation is carried out after the dues are cleared through the platform. If Offline Payment is selected, the institution handles the external payment process and assigns rooms accordingly.<br>Auto Allotment: Under this allotment type, rooms are allocated to students based on their payment status. When Dues Management is selected, students can select their preferred room, and the corresponding dues are generated. The rooms are allotted automatically once the dues are cleared. Auto Allotment does not apply when the payment method is Offline.<br>This setup ensures that room allocation is aligned with institutional policies while providing flexibility in payment and allotment processes. | The Hostel Management Configuration feature is essential as it streamlines the process of room allocation and fee payment tracking, ensuring operational efficiency and transparency. By providing institutions the flexibility to choose between Dues Management and Offline Payment, this feature accommodates various payment preferences while ensuring that payment statuses are accurately tracked. This is crucial for institutions to manage hostel operations smoothly and maintain financial accountability. |     |

The platform allows you to select all programs or use a checkbox to select multiple programs based on preference.<br><br>Batch Year: The specific academic year or batch of students the policy option applies to, ensuring room allocation aligns with the academic cycle. The platform allows you to select all batch years or use a checkbox to select multiple batch years based on preference.<br><br>Quota: A specific category under which students are assigned to the hostel, such as general, NRI, SC/ST, or others.
