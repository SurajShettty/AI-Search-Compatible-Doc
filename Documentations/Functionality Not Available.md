# Functionality Not Available

This document outlines current features and functionalities that are not available or possible in the Hostel Management Configuration in the Digiicampus platform.


**Editing or Archiving or Deleting Hostel Policies**\n Once a policy is created, it cannot be edited, archived, or deleted, limiting flexibility for necessary adjustments. If there are any changes required, a new policy must be created.



---


**Multiple Active Policies**\n The platform only allows one active policy at a time. This prevents institutions from running multiple policies simultaneously, which would be helpful in cases of different departments or special provisions for various student groups.



---


**Editing Hostel Policy Options**\n Once a policy option is created and activated, it cannot be modified. Any required changes to fees, dates, or options would require creating a new policy, increasing administrative workload.



---


**Automatic Policy Option End Functionality**\n Although a **Policy Option End Date** can be set, the platform does not automatically cancel room allotments after the policy ends. **Hostel Administrators** must manually cancel room allocations, increasing administrative effort.



---


**Modifying Room Capacity After Allocation**\n Once rooms are allotted, their capacity cannot be changed in the **Infrastructure Module**. This restricts flexibility in managing room allocation adjustments after the initial assignment, even if there is a need to change the capacity.



---


**Bulk Upload for Hostel Policy Option Entries**\n The platform requires **Hostel Policy Options** to be added individually, which increases the workload for the **Hostel Administrator** when a large number of options need to be configured.
