## Add Hostel Policy

<!-- id: adding-hostel-policy | category: Policy Management -->

**What it does**

This feature enables the creation of new policies for hostel fees, allowing administrators to define specific rules and structures for different academic years or student groups.

**Why it matters**

Establishing clear hostel policies is crucial for transparent fee collection, managing student expectations, and ensuring consistent application of rules across the hostel system.

**How to use**

1. Step 1: Navigate to Hostel Fee.
2. Step 2: Click on Add Policy to create a new policy.

**Examples**

- Example: A policy named 'AY 2025-26' with policy code 'AY2526' and a status of 'Active' can be created.

## Add Hostel Policy Option

<!-- id: adding-hostel-policy-option | category: Policy Management -->

**What it does**

This feature allows administrators to add detailed options to an existing hostel policy, specifying parameters such as option name, code, fee amount, associated department, program, batch year, quota, gender, room type, and allotment start and end dates.

**Why it matters**

Adding policy options provides granular control and flexibility, enabling the customisation of hostel offerings to cater to diverse student demographics, academic programs, and accommodation preferences.

**How to use**

1. Step 1: Navigate to Hostel Policy, select an existing active policy, and click on Add Option.
2. Step 2: Click on Add Option, and enter the required details.

**Examples**

- An example policy option could be 'Undergraduate - Female Dorm' (Option Code: UG-FEM-101) with an Option Fee Amount of 2000, applicable to 'All Departments' for 'Undergraduate' programs in '2023' Batch Year, with a 'General' Quota for 'Female' gender, 'Single Room' type, and an Allotment Start Date of '2023-10-01' and End Date of '2024-09-30'.
