## Offline Payment Room Allocation

<!-- id: offline-payment-room-allocation | category: Hostel Management -->

**What it does**

When a student selects Offline Payment, room allocation is not automatic. The institution must manually confirm the payment externally and then manually allocate rooms within the system.

**Why it matters**

This clarifies the manual process required for offline payments, preventing confusion and ensuring rooms are assigned correctly after external payment verification.

**How to use**

1. Confirm the offline payment externally (e.g., bank transfer, cash).
2. Manually allocate rooms to the student in the system.

**Examples**

- A student pays their hostel fees via bank transfer. The hostel administrator confirms the payment with the bank and then manually assigns a room in the system.

## Room Allocation Delay for Non-Dues Management Payments

<!-- id: delay-room-allocation-no-dues-management | category: Hostel Management -->

**What it does**

If hostel fees are not paid through Dues Management, room allocation will be delayed until the payment is manually processed and confirmed by the institution.

**Why it matters**

This clarifies the consequences of not using the Dues Management system for payments, encouraging its use for faster allocation and setting proper expectations for alternative methods.

**How to use**

1. If not using Dues Management, ensure your payment is processed and confirmed externally.
2. Await manual room allocation after confirmation.

**Examples**

- A student makes an offline payment. Their room is not immediately allocated; it will be assigned only after the finance department manually confirms the payment and updates the system, which can take time.
