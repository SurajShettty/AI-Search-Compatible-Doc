## Add Menu in Bulk

<!-- id: add-menu-in-bulk | category: Menu Management -->

**What it does**

This feature allows administrators to efficiently upload multiple meal menus for a selected mess using a CSV (Comma Separated Values) template, streamlining the process of updating daily or weekly meal plans.

**Why it matters**

Adding menus in bulk saves significant time and reduces manual data entry errors compared to adding each meal item individually, especially for messes with extensive and frequently changing menus.

**How to use**

1. Step 1- Navigate to the Mess Management section.
2. Step 2- Select the mess for which the menu needs to be uploaded.
3. Step 3- Click on the Add Menu in Bulk option.
4. Step 4- Download the CSV template to enter the meal menu data.
5. Step 5- Fill in the required menu data in the CSV template.
6. Step 6- Upload the completed CSV file to add the menu to the platform.

**Examples**

- A mess administrator downloads the CSV template and fills it with breakfast, lunch, and dinner items for October 1st, 2nd, and 3rd, including details like 'Toast (Veg)', 'Chicken Curry (Non-Veg)', 'Pulao (Veg)', and 'Fish Fry (Non-Veg)'.

## Add Members in Bulk

<!-- id: add-members-in-bulk | category: Member Management -->

**Why it matters**

Bulk member addition significantly reduces the administrative burden and time required for enrolling a large number of individuals into a mess, ensuring efficient onboarding.

**How to use**

1. Step 1- Navigate to the Mess Management section.
2. Step 2- Select the mess to which the members need to be added.
3. Step 3- Click on the members tab and click on the Bulk Upload Members option.
4. Step 4: Download the CSV template and enter the required member data.
5. Step 5: Upload the completed CSV template to add the members.

**Examples**

- An administrator needs to add 50 new students to the mess. They download the CSV template, fill in the Registration IDs, Email IDs, and set 'Coupon Generation Status' to 'Active' or 'Inactive' for each student, then upload the file.

## Manage Individual Mess Members

<!-- id: add-remove-activate-deactivate-members | category: Member Management -->

**What it does**

This feature provides granular control over mess membership, allowing administrators to individually add, remove, activate, or deactivate specific members from a chosen mess.

**Why it matters**

Individual member management is crucial for handling specific cases like new enrollments, withdrawals, temporary suspensions, or reactivations, ensuring accurate mess access and billing.

**How to use**

1. 1. Navigate to the Admin Console.
2. 2. Go to Mess Management.
3. 3. Click on the specific Mess.
4. 4. Go to the Members section.
5. 5. Click on the Action button.
6. 6. Select the appropriate action:
7.    * Add Member to add a new member.
8.    * Remove Member to remove an existing member.
9.    * Activate to restore meal access for a member.
10.    * Deactivate to suspend meal access for a member.

**Examples**

- A student leaves the campus, so the administrator navigates to their mess and selects 'Remove Member'. Later, another student returns from leave, and the administrator 'Activates' their mess access.

## Manage Mess Groups

<!-- id: add-remove-groups | category: Group Management -->

**What it does**

This feature allows administrators to create and delete predefined groups within a mess, categorizing members based on criteria such as hostel, allotment status (Confirmed or Provisional), and gender.

**Why it matters**

Managing members in groups simplifies the assignment and oversight of mess services for specific cohorts, such as all students from a particular hostel or all confirmed female residents.

**How to use**

1. 1. Navigate to the Admin Console.
2. 2. Go to Mess Management.
3. 3. Click on the specific Mess.
4. 4. Go to the Members section.
5. 5. Go to Groups.
6. 6. To Add New Group, click on Add New Group.
7. 7. Select the Hostel, Allotment (Confirmed or Provisional), and Gender.
8. 8. Click Add Group to save the new group.
9. 9. To Remove Group, select the group and click Remove.

**Examples**

- An administrator creates a new group for 'Hostel A, Confirmed Allotment, Female' students to manage their mess access collectively. Later, an outdated group for 'Provisional Allotment' is removed.

## Enter Meal Coupon

<!-- id: enter-meal-coupon | category: Coupon Management -->

**What it does**

This feature allows a Mess Administrator, with appropriate privileges, to process meal coupons by either manually entering a coupon code on the web interface or scanning a QR code using the DigiiCampus Mobile Application.

**Why it matters**

Efficient coupon entry and redemption are vital for managing meal services, ensuring that users with valid coupons can avail their meals seamlessly and that coupon usage is accurately tracked.

**How to use**

1. 1. Navigate to the Admin Console.
2. 2. Go to Mess Management.
3. 3. Go to Avail Coupon.
4. 4. With Mess Console Admin privileges, the Mess Administrator can enter the coupon code to allow users to avail the coupon.
5.    * On the web, simply enter the coupon code.
6.    * In the DigiiCampus Mobile Application, scan the QR code of the coupon.

**Examples**

- A student presents a meal coupon. The Mess Administrator either types the 'coupon code' into the web portal or uses the DigiiCampus Mobile Application to 'scan the QR code' on the coupon to validate and redeem it.
