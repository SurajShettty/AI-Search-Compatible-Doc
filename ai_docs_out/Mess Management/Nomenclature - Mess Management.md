**Tags:** mess, dining, food service, management, administration, campus, students, staff, guests, meals, coupons, reports, configuration, access, nomenclature


## Overview

This document outlines the key nomenclature and features within the Digii campus-management platform's Mess Management module. It details functionalities ranging from administrative access and mess configuration to meal scheduling, menu management, member enrollment, and meal coupon redemption, providing a comprehensive guide for managing dining services.

## Prerequisites

- **Digii Platform Access** — Users must have access to the Digii campus-management platform.
- **Administrative Privileges** — Specific features require appropriate administrative roles, such as Super Admin or Mess Administrator.
- **Infrastructure Module Configuration** — For the 'Location' feature, building details must be previously included in the Infrastructure module.

## Meal Addition

<!-- id: meal-addition | category: Menu Management -->

**What it does**

This feature allows the mess administrator to add meals to the menu using two methods: a visual Calendar View for day-by-day entry (distinguishing veg/non-veg meals) and a Bulk Upload via CSV template for efficient entry of multiple meals. Only future meal data can be added, and only the current day's meals are visible to users on their home screen.

**Why it matters**

It provides flexible and efficient ways to update meal menus, ensuring members are informed of current options while maintaining control over future menu visibility and streamlining administrative tasks.

**How to use**

1. **Calendar View**: Select a specific date on the calendar and add meals based on meal types (e.g., veg or non-veg). Meals are marked red for non-veg and green for veg meals.
2. **Add Menu (Bulk Upload via CSV)**: Download a bulk CSV template including fields like Meal, Date, Item1, Type1, Item2, Type2. Enter the data into the template and upload the file for efficient bulk meal entry.

**Examples**

- Adding 'Paneer Butter Masala (Veg)' and 'Chicken Curry (Non-Veg)' for Tuesday's lunch via Calendar View.
- Uploading a CSV file to add a full month's menu for all meal types.

## Add Member to Mess

<!-- id: add-member-to-mess | category: Member Management -->

**What it does**

The Mess Management module allows mess administrators to add members (students, staff, guests) through three methods: individually by entering details, in bulk using a CSV template, or in groups by selecting criteria such as hostel, allotment status, and gender.

**Why it matters**

It streamlines the process of enrolling individuals and groups into mess services, ensuring efficient and organized member management, especially during periods of high enrollment.

**How to use**

1. **Individual Member Addition**: Enter details such as Registration ID, Email ID, and phone number.
2. **Bulk Member Upload**: Download a CSV template with fields like Registration ID, Email ID, and Coupon Generation Status. Enter the data and upload the file.
3. **Group Addition**: Select a hostel, choose the allotment status (Confirmed/Provisional), and gender (Female/Male/Other). Click 'Add' to automatically add the group.

**Examples**

- Adding a new student individually using their Registration ID.
- Uploading a CSV of all new hostel residents for bulk enrollment.
- Adding all 'Confirmed' female residents from 'Hostel B' as a group.
