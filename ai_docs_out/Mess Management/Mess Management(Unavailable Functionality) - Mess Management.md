# Mess Management (Unavailable Functionality)

**Module:** Mess Management  
**Tags:** Mess Management, Unavailable Functionality, Limitations, Future Features, Meal Planning, Food Services, System Gaps


## Overview

This document outlines several functionalities currently unavailable within the Digii Mess Management module. It details limitations regarding the deletion of added messes, viewing past or future meal menus, personal meal consumption tracking for students and staff, the ability to add or modify previous meal menus, and options for users to select preferred meals to reduce wastage.

## Inability to Delete Added Mess

<!-- id: inability-to-delete-added-mess | category: Mess Configuration -->

**What it does**

Currently, the platform allows mess administrators to add or modify mess details but does not provide an option to delete a mess once created.

**Why it matters**

This restriction prevents the removal of outdated or unused mess data from the platform, leading to potential data clutter and inaccuracy if a mess is no longer operational or has been converted.

## Inability to Add or Modify Previous Meal Menus

## User-Specific Meal Preference Selection

<!-- id: user-meal-preference-selection | category: Meal Customization -->

**What it does**

The platform displays the meal menu per meal type but does not allow individual members (students or staff) to select their preferred meals.

**Why it matters**

This leads to potential food wastage as members may be served meals they do not want or need, and it prevents personalization, making it harder for mess administrators to match meal quantities with actual demand and promote a sustainable meal system.
