## Create New Mess

<!-- id: create-new-mess | category: Mess Administration -->

**What it does**

This feature allows a Mess Administrator to establish a new mess by entering essential details such as the mess name, location, and specific meal timings for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

**Why it matters**

It is crucial for setting up new dining facilities within the campus management system, enabling the provision of meal services to students and staff.

**How to use**

1. Enter the desired mess name.
2. Specify the mess location.
3. Define meal timings for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

**Examples**

- A Mess Administrator creates 'Main Campus Mess' at 'Building A, Ground Floor' with breakfast from 7-9 AM, lunch from 12-2 PM, and dinner from 7-9 PM.

## Expired Meal Coupons

<!-- id: expired-meal-coupons | category: Meal Consumption -->

**What it does**

If a student or staff member attempts to avail a meal after its designated timing has ended, their meal coupon will automatically expire, rendering it unusable for that meal.

**Why it matters**

This mechanism enforces meal schedules, prevents unauthorized or late meal consumption, and ensures fair usage and operational efficiency of the mess services.

**Examples**

- A student tries to scan their meal coupon for lunch at 2:30 PM, but the lunch timing ended at 2:00 PM. The coupon is marked as expired, and the student cannot avail the meal.

## Impact of Modified Mess Meal Timings

<!-- id: modified-mess-meal-timings-impact | category: Meal Management -->

**What it does**

When mess meal timings are modified, the updated timings are immediately visible on the front end. However, the ability to avail meal coupons based on these revised timings only takes effect from the next day onward, ensuring that meals already planned or consumed for the current day are not impacted.

**Why it matters**

This approach ensures operational stability and prevents confusion or issues with meal coupons for the current day when timing changes are made, maintaining consistency for ongoing meal services.

**Examples**

- If lunch timings are changed from 12-2 PM to 1-3 PM on Monday, the new timings will be visible immediately. However, students can only avail coupons for the 1-3 PM slot starting from Tuesday; Monday's coupons are still valid for the original 12-2 PM slot.
