# Related Workflows

**Module:** Asset Management  
**Tags:** workflow, asset management, custom workflow, automation, configuration, fixed asset, consumable asset


## Overview

This document outlines common workflows related to asset management, such as gate movement, repair, requisition, disposal, calibration, maintenance, and warranty requests. These workflows are customisable and can be configured by the Asset Administration team within the Workflow Automation module.

## Prerequisites

- **Workflow Automation module** — Required for configuring custom asset workflows.
- **Asset Administration team** — Responsible for configuring and managing asset-related workflows.

## Asset Gate Movement Request

<!-- id: asset-gate-movement-request | category: Fixed Asset Management -->

**What it does**

Allows staff or faculty to request permission to move an asset out of campus, generating an asset gatepass. This workflow is customisable and configured by the Asset Administration team.

**Why it matters**

Ensures proper tracking and authorisation for assets leaving the campus, enhancing security and accountability for valuable institutional property.

**How to use**

1. 1. The Asset Administration team configures this custom workflow in the Workflow Automation module.
2. 2. Staff or Faculty then submit the Asset Gate Movement Request workform, providing details like asset name, code, description, and reason for taking the asset out of campus.

**Examples**

- A lab technician needs to take a piece of equipment off-campus for repair and submits a gate movement request.
- A staff member needs to move a projector to an external event and requires an approved asset gatepass.
